About Me

I am interested in developing better abstractions for quantum computation: physics-informed abstractions that are expressive enough for algorithm design, rigorous enough for automatic analysis and synthesis, comprehensible enough for humans, and practical enough for efficient compilation. My broader interests lie at the intersection of programming languages, formal semantics, and quantum physics.
